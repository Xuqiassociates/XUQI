Building Regulations

Technical drawings for practical, coordinated construction.

We prepare building regulations drawings and technical information to help residential projects move from design intent towards a clear and buildable route.

What we do

Clear technical information for approval and construction.

Building regulations drawings translate the approved or developed design into technical information that can be reviewed, coordinated and built. They help clarify construction build-ups, dimensions, structural requirements and practical details.

XUQI Associates supports homeowners and small developers with technical drawing packages for extensions, renovations, loft conversions and structural alterations, helping each project progress with greater clarity.

01

Technical drawings

We prepare plans, sections, elevations and details to communicate how the project can be constructed.

02

Construction details

We develop key details for walls, roofs, floors, openings and junctions to support technical review and coordination.

03

Structural coordination

We coordinate architectural information with structural design, including beams, openings and load paths where required.

04

Submission support

We help organise the technical drawing package so it can be used for building control review and project coordination.

Our process

A structured route from design proposal to technical package.

Step 01

Review

We review the existing information, proposed design, planning status and any known structural or construction constraints.

Step 02

Coordinate

We coordinate the architectural layout with structural requirements, key dimensions, openings, levels and practical construction considerations.

Step 03

Detail

We prepare technical drawings and details that explain the construction approach, material build-ups and important junctions.

Step 04

Package

We organise the final technical information into a clear drawing package for review, coordination and the next stage of the project.
