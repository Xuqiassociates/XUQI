Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how XUQI Associates collects, uses, and protects any personal information you provide when you use our website or submit an enquiry.

We are committed to ensuring that your privacy is protected and that all personal data is handled in accordance with UK GDPR regulations.

Information we may collect

  • Your name and contact details, such as email address and phone number
  • Project information you choose to provide
  • Location details, such as postcode or property address
  • Technical data, such as IP address, browser type and website usage information

How we use your information

  • To respond to enquiries and provide quotations
  • To deliver architectural and structural engineering services

Data security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, disclosure, loss or misuse.

To exercise your rights, please contact us at contact@xuqiassociates.co.uk.
