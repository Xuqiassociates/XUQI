Structural Engineering

Clear structural thinking for safe, practical and buildable projects.

We provide structural engineering design, calculations and reports for extensions, alterations, refurbishments and structural assessments across the UK.

What we do

Structural support that connects design intent with technical certainty.

Structural engineering plays a key role in helping a project move safely from design idea to construction. We assess how loads are carried, how existing buildings behave, and how new structural elements can be introduced clearly and efficiently.

XUQI Associates supports homeowners, designers, contractors and property professionals with structural calculations, drawings, inspections and reports, helping projects respond to both building control requirements and practical construction needs.

Core expertise

Engineering input for structural alterations and new works.

01

02

Load-bearing wall removal

Our process

A precise and practical route through structural design.

Step 01

Review

We review your drawings, photos, site information, proposed works and any known structural concerns.

Step 02

Assess

We assess the existing structure, load paths, constraints and the likely structural strategy for the project.

Step 03

Design

We prepare the structural calculations, drawings and technical notes required for coordination and review.

Step 04

Support

We help respond to technical queries from building control, contractors or other project stakeholders where required.
