Our process

A precise and practical route through structural design.

Step 01

Review

We review drawings, photographs, site information, proposed works and any known structural concerns.

Step 02

Assess

We assess the existing structure, load paths, constraints and the likely structural strategy.

Step 03

Design

We prepare calculations, drawings and technical notes required for review and coordination.

Step 04

Support

We help respond to building control, contractor or project team queries where required.
