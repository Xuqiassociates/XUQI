Architectural Design

Calm, considered design for homes, extensions and renovations.

We develop clear architectural proposals that respond to the existing building, the site context and the way each client wants to live or work.

What we do

Design support from early ideas to a clear architectural direction.

Good architectural design starts with understanding the property, the client’s needs and the practical limits of planning, structure and construction. We help translate early ideas into spatial proposals that are calm, clear and buildable.

Whether you are planning a rear extension, a loft conversion, an internal reconfiguration or a new-build home, we prepare design information that helps the project move forward with confidence.

01

Initial design review

We review the property, client brief, site context and key constraints to establish a practical design direction.

02

Concept design

We develop early layout options, massing ideas and spatial strategies to test how the project could work.

03

Existing and proposed drawings

We prepare clear plans, elevations and sections to communicate the design for planning, discussion and coordination.

04

Design coordination

We coordinate architectural thinking with structural requirements, helping the design remain elegant and buildable.

Our approach

A clear process for turning ideas into workable proposals.

Step 01

Understand

We begin by understanding your property, lifestyle, budget direction, timescale and the type of architectural support you need.

Step 02

Explore

We test spatial options and design possibilities, considering light, circulation, proportion, structure and planning context.

Step 03

Develop

We refine the preferred option into a clear architectural proposal, supported by drawings and design information.

Step 04

Coordinate

We help align the design with planning and structural considerations, creating a more practical route towards the next project stage.
