Planning Applications

Clear planning drawings for thoughtful residential projects.

We prepare planning drawings and supporting information for extensions, renovations and new-build homes, helping each project respond clearly to its site, local context and planning requirements.

What we do

From early ideas to a complete planning submission.

A successful planning application needs more than a set of drawings. It needs a clear design approach, careful understanding of the site, and documents that allow the proposal to be assessed with confidence.

XUQI Associates supports homeowners, developers and small commercial clients with planning-stage drawings and coordinated design information, creating a practical route from initial concept to submission.

01

Existing drawings

We prepare clear existing plans, elevations and site information to establish the current condition of the property.

02

Proposed design drawings

We develop proposed plans, elevations and sections that communicate the design clearly for planning review.

03

Planning support documents

Where required, we assist with design statements, visual material and supporting information for the application.

04

Submission guidance

We help organise the application package and guide clients through the information usually needed for submission.

Our process

A calm and structured route through planning.

Step 01

Initial conversation

We discuss your property, your aims, the type of work you want to do, and any known planning constraints.

Step 02

Site review

We review the site, surrounding context and relevant design considerations before developing the drawing package.

Step 03

Drawing preparation

Existing and proposed drawings are prepared with a focus on clarity, proportion and planning readability.

Step 04

Planning package

We coordinate the final planning information so it can be submitted clearly and professionally.
